# citizenM Check-in SOP

## Early Check-in
Guests may be welcomed before the 15:00 standard check-in time when a room in the
booked category is already cleaned and released by Housekeeping. Ambassadors may
grant up to 3 hours early at their discretion; earlier arrivals are held in the
living room with a welcome drink and escalated to the Manager on Duty. Never
release a room that Housekeeping has not confirmed clean in Apaleo.

## Room Assignment
Every arriving reservation is assigned a unit from the booked unit group. An
Ambassador may assign a single room themselves once Housekeeping has released it
as clean in Apaleo and the unit carries no rate-affecting upgrade. Never assign a
unit that Housekeeping has not confirmed, and never assign out of the booked group
to solve an availability problem — that is an upgrade, and it is governed as one.

A party booking more than one room is not a series of single assignments. Families
and groups travelling together are placed on the same floor wherever the house
allows it, and adjacent where it does not; where a party includes children the
rooms must be adjacent or directly opposite, never split across floors. Because
holding a floor for one party affects every other arrival that day, a multi-room
assignment is a Manager on Duty decision — the Ambassador prepares the proposal
and the MoD weighs it against the arrivals board. The same applies to a single
room when a same-floor or adjacency request on the booking cannot be met from the
units actually free: the guest has asked for something the inventory will not give,
and a human decides what to offer instead rather than the agent quietly ignoring
the request.

Record the party size, the requested adjacency and the floor chosen with every
multi-room assignment. An allocation without a recorded reason cannot be reviewed
when the next group arrives.

## Room Upgrade at Check-in
An upgrade is a hospitality gesture, not an entitlement. Ambassadors may offer a
complimentary upgrade when the nightly uplift is modest and inventory is
genuinely spare for the whole stay. Higher-value upgrades — suites, long stays,
or when the property is filling — are a Manager on Duty decision. Loyalty and
service-recovery context should be weighed first.

## Registration and Identity Capture
Every arriving guest must have a complete registration record before a key is
issued: full name, contact, and — where the local jurisdiction requires it — a
captured identity document. Incomplete registrations are corrected at the desk;
a key is never issued against a missing mandatory field.

## Pre-authorisation and Payment on File
A valid payment method must be confirmed on the folio at arrival. The standard
incidentals pre-authorisation is held per the property's policy. Ambassadors
validate standard pre-authorisations; unusually high holds, corporate-billing
exceptions, or VIP arrangements are confirmed by the Manager on Duty.

## Key and Mobile Access Issuance
Keys and mobile access are issued only once the guest is checked in (InHouse) and
payment is validated. Access is scoped to the guest's room and stay dates.

## Rate Override Below the Best Available Rate
A guest, corporate account or travel partner may ask for a rate below the Best
Available Rate for the dates requested. An Ambassador may apply a discount up to the
front-line limit set in the Exception Authority when the rate plan is not a negotiated
or corporate agreement. Beyond that limit the request goes to the Revenue Manager, who
weighs it against the property's rate strategy for those dates. Record the commercial
reason — corporate account, service recovery, occupancy — with every override; a
discount without a recorded reason cannot be reviewed later.

## Booking Outside Rate-plan or Availability Rules
Rate-plan restrictions, minimum-advance rules and availability closures exist to protect
revenue and operations, so they are never set aside by the front line and never by the
agent acting alone. A request to book or amend through one of these restrictions is a
force-manage action: it always goes to a human, at every phase of the agent's operation,
and the specific restriction being overridden must be named in the request before anyone
is asked to approve it. Approval is recorded against the named restriction, never as a
general permission.
